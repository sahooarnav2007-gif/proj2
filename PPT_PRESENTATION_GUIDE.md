**Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement SIH26135**  
*Sponsored by the Government of Maharashtra (MSIS / Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship & Innovation)*

---

## 📌 1. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch (What is this project?)
> *"When students complete government skilling courses (like ITI or PMKVY in Maharashtra), the government knows who passed, but **loses contact with 80% of them after 6 months**. The government has no idea who actually got a job, who started a business, or who got a salary hike.  
> **Skill Sync** is an AI-powered tracking platform that keeps in touch with trainees for **3 years** via **WhatsApp Bots & Marathi Voice Calls**, verifies their employment through **EPFO/Udyam & Employers**, and uses **AI to predict who might quit their job** so counselors can help them."*

---

## 🚨 2. The Problem We Are Solving (The "Before" Scenario)
- **Problem Statement**: `SIH26135` (Smart India Hackathon 2026)
- **Client**: Government of Maharashtra (*Maharashtra State Innovation Society - MSIS / Dept of Skills*)
- **Category**: Software | **Theme**: Smart Education

### Key Flaws in Current System:
1. **Phone Number Churn**: Trainees change SIM cards or migrate $\rightarrow$ 80% follow-up contact loss.
2. **Employer Reporting Inertia**: Companies don't report back on hiring/retention outcomes.
3. **Informal & Self-Employment Blindness**: Freelancers and small micro-entrepreneurs are marked as "unemployed".
4. **No Longitudinal View (3, 6, 12, 24, 36 Months)**: Government cannot measure wage progression or skilling ROI over time.
5. **Outdated Syllabus**: Trainees learn on old machines and quit due to low wages.

---

## 💡 3. Our 4 Core Innovations (The Solution)

1. **🤖 Multi-Channel Automated Re-Engagement (No App Download Needed)**:
   - **WhatsApp Bot**: Interactive button messages in Marathi, Hindi, and English to update salary and company in 10 seconds.
   - **Marathi AI Voice Call (IVR)**: For rural youth on basic 2G/3G keypad phones (e.g. *Gadchiroli/Nandurbar*).
   - **SkillCoins Rewards**: Trainees earn reward points for updating their career status!

2. **🔍 3-Way Signal Triangulation (100% Verified Truth)**:
   - Eliminates fake placement data by cross-verifying:
     `[Trainee Report]` + `[Employer 1-Click Approval]` + `[EPFO / Udyam Registry Token]` = **Verified Trust Score**.

3. **🧠 AI Predictive Attrition Early Warning**:
   - Machine learning model flags trainees with high commute-to-wage stress (e.g. traveling 30km for ₹13,000) so counselors can intervene **before** the trainee quits.

4. **🔍 NLP Curriculum Skill-Gap Mining**:
   - AI extracts feedback from employers and exit calls to find outdated machinery (e.g. manual lathe vs. 5-axis CNC; EV battery BMS diagnostics) and recommends syllabus updates to the state government.

5. **🔒 DPDP Act 2023 Privacy Vault**:
   - Masked Aadhaar numbers, tokenized citizen IDs, and revocable consent.
