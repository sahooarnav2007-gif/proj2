--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 30-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH (WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When students complete government skilling courses (like ITI or PMKVY in Maharashtra), 
the government knows who passed, but LOSES CONTACT WITH 80% OF THEM after 6 months. 
The government has no idea who actually got a job, who started a business, or who 
got a salary hike.

Skill Sync is an AI-powered tracking platform that keeps in touch with trainees for 
3 YEARS via automated WhatsApp Bots and Marathi Voice Calls (IVR), verifies their 
employment through EPFO Provident Fund / Udyam MSME and Employers, and uses AI to 
predict who might quit their job so counselors can help them before drop-out occurs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PROBLEM (WHY WAS THIS BUILT?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Problem Statement: SIH26135 (Smart India Hackathon 2026)
• Organization: Government of Maharashtra (Maharashtra State Innovation Society - MSIS)
• Category: Software | Theme: Smart Education

Key Flaws in Today's System:
1. Phone Number Churn: Trainees change SIM cards or migrate -> 80% contact loss.
2. Employer Reporting Inertia: Companies rarely report who stayed or resigned.
3. Informal/Self-Employment Blindness: Freelancers and small shop owners are ignored.
4. No Long-Term (Longitudinal) View: No visibility on 3, 6, 12, 24, 36-month wage growth.
5. Outdated Syllabus: Trainees learn on old machines and quit due to low wages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OUR 4 CORE INNOVATIONS (THE SOLUTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MULTI-CHANNEL AUTOMATED RE-ENGAGEMENT (Zero App Download Needed):
   - WhatsApp Bot with interactive buttons in Marathi, Hindi, and English.
   - Marathi AI Voice Call (IVR) for rural youth on basic 2G/3G keypad phones.
   - SkillCoins Rewards: Trainees earn reward coins for updating their job status!

2. 3-WAY SIGNAL TRIANGULATION (100% Verified Truth):
   - We eliminate fake placement claims by cross-verifying:
     [Trainee Report] + [Employer 1-Click Approval] + [EPFO/Udyam Registry Token]
     = Verified Trust Score.

3. PREDICTIVE AI ATTRITION EARLY WARNING:
   - Machine learning model flags trainees with high commute-to-wage stress 
     (e.g., traveling 30km for Rs 13,000) so counselors intervene BEFORE they quit.

4. NLP CURRICULUM SKILL-GAP MINING:
   - AI extracts feedback from employers and exit calls to find outdated machinery 
     (e.g., manual lathe vs 5-axis CNC; EV battery BMS diagnostics) and recommends 
     syllabus updates to the Maharashtra government.

5. DPDP ACT 2023 PRIVACY VAULT:
   - Fully compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
   - Aadhaar numbers are masked, citizen data is tokenized, and consent is revocable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE 4 USER ROLES IN THE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STATE OFFICIAL (Govt of Maharashtra / MSIS):
   - State-wide KPI dashboard across all 36 districts.
   - Longitudinal retention curves (Month 3 to 36).
   - Training Provider ROI Leaderboard and policy budget recommendations.

2. TRAINING PROVIDER (ITI Principal / Center Head):
   - Cohort tracking schedule (Day 90, 180, 365, 730).
   - Early warning queue for high-risk drop-out candidates.
   - 1-click batch re-engagement campaign dispatcher.

3. EMPLOYER (HR & Factory Managers):
   - 1-click verification queue to confirm employee salary and active status.
   - Direct skill deficiency feedback submission to state curriculum boards.

4. TRAINEE (Student / Alumni):
   - Career milestone timeline (showing wage progression e.g. Rs 14k -> Rs 34k).
   - SkillCoins wallet & DPDP consent management toggles.
