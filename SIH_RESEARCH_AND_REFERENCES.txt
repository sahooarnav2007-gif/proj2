2. GOVERNMENT OF INDIA & NITI AAYOG POLICY RESEARCH REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NITI Aayog (2022) — "Evaluation of National Skill Development Ecosystem and Post-Training Employment Retention."
   - Citation: NITI Aayog Governance Series, Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office (DMEO), New Delhi.
   - Relevance: Highlights that 72% to 80% of vocational trainees cannot be traced 6 months post-certification due to mobile number churn and unrecorded migration.

2. Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) (2023) — "Comprehensive Tracer Study of ITI and PMKVY Graduates in Western India."
   - Citation: MSDE Research Bulletin, Directorate General of Training (DGT), Govt of India.
   - Relevance: Identifies that traditional single-point phone survey methodologies have an attrition error margin of >65% compared to multi-channel conversational follow-up.

3. Standing Committee on Labour, Textiles and Skill Development (Lok Sabha, 2023-24) — "Report on Post-Placement Support and Longitudinal Wage Progression Monitoring."
   - Citation: Seventeenth Lok Sabha Report No. 42, Parliament of India.
   - Relevance: Recommends integration of EPFO/ESIC data as a non-intrusive triangulation proxy to confirm formal employment retention.

4. Planning Department, Government of Maharashtra (2024-25) — "Economic Survey of Maharashtra: Chapter on Employment and Vocational Skilling Outcomes."
   - Citation: Directorate of Economics and Statistics, Mantralaya, Mumbai.
   - Relevance: Outlines district-level variations in placement ratios across Vidarbha, Marathwada, and Western Maharashtra industrial zones.

3. ACADEMIC & INTERNATIONAL RESEARCH PAPERS (ILO, WORLD BANK, NCAER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. International Labour Organization (ILO) (2020) — "Carrying Out Tracer Studies in Technical and Vocational Education and Training: Methodological Guide."
   - Citation: ILO TVET Research Manual, Geneva, ISBN 978-92-2-032544-2.
   - Relevance: Establishes the 3M, 6M, 12M, 24M, and 36M longitudinal tracking milestones used in Skill Sync's data cadence.

2. World Bank Policy Research (2021) — "From Training to Livelihood: Tracking Labor Market Trajectories and Wage Growth in Emerging Economies."
   - Citation: World Bank Group Education Global Practice, Policy Research Working Paper No. 9412.
   - Relevance: Validates that measuring wage multipliers (starting stipend vs 24-month earnings) is 3x more predictive of training program ROI than initial placement rates alone.

3. National Council of Applied Economic Research (NCAER) (2022) — "Skilling India: Evaluating Impact, Wage Multipliers, and Shopfloor Skill Deficiencies."
   - Citation: NCAER Labour and Education Studies Monograph, New Delhi.
   - Relevance: Provides the empirical foundation for Skill Sync's NLP topic clustering to identify shopfloor machinery gaps (CNC, EV battery systems).

4. Schomburg, H. & Teichler, U. (2018) — "Longitudinal Employment Outcome Analysis and Higher Vocational Impact Assessment."
   - Citation: Higher Education and Labour Market Research, Springer Academic Publications.
   - Relevance: Standard framework for conversational multi-lingual survey design without survey fatigue.

4. STATUTORY ACTS, PRIVACY REGULATIONS & LEGAL CITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023)
   - Legal Reference: Act No. 22 of 2023, Ministry of Law and Justice, Gazette of India.
   - Relevant Provisions: Section 6 (Consent Notice & Purpose Limitation), Section 7 (Legitimate government welfare tracking), and Section 8 (Data Principal Rights).
   - Implementation in Skill Sync: Purpose-bound revocable consent ledger with tokenized identifier hashes.

2. The Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 (as amended)
   - Legal Reference: UIDAI Circular on Masked Aadhaar & Virtual ID (VID) Regulations (2018).
   - Implementation in Skill Sync: Complete pseudonymization — only masked Aadhaar (XXXXXXXX7821) and Virtual Identifiers are stored.

3. The Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006 & Udyam Notification (2020)
   - Legal Reference: Gazette Notification S.O. 2119(E), Ministry of MSME.
   - Implementation in Skill Sync: Verification of self-employed micro-entrepreneurs via Udyam Registration Number (URN).

4. The Apprentices Act, 1961 (amended 2014) & NAPS Operational Framework
   - Legal Reference: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Directorate General of Training.
   - Implementation in Skill Sync: Validation of on-the-job apprenticeship contracts and DBT stipend records.
