[SLIDE 1 & 2: TITLE & THE PROBLEM]

"Respected Judges and Evaluators, good morning. 

Every year, the Government of Maharashtra invests hundreds of crores to train lakhs 
of youth through ITIs, PMKVA, and state skilling initiatives. Our current portals 
capture enrollment, classroom attendance, and certification with great precision.

BUT THE MOMENT A STUDENT GRADUATES, THE GOVERNMENT LOSES CONTACT WITH OVER 80% OF 
THEM WITHIN 6 MONTHS. 

Let me give you a real-world example. Meet Swapnil, a 20-year-old student from rural 
Nashik who completed an ITI certification in EV maintenance and CNC machining. On 
graduation day, the government marked him as 'successfully skilled'. 

But within 3 months:
1. Swapnil changed his SIM card and migrated to Pune for work. When government officials 
   called his old phone number, it was switched off.
2. The training institute claimed on paper that 90% of students were placed, but the state 
   had zero proof of whether Swapnil was actually working or what salary he made.
3. Swapnil faced long travel hours and night shifts on low pay. He had no counselor to turn 
   to, got burned out, and silently quit his job.
4. His factory manager noticed Swapnil lacked modern EV battery diagnostic skills, but had 
   no easy channel to tell the state government in Mantralaya to update the curriculum.

To eliminate this data black hole, our team built SKILL SYNC — an AI-powered longitudinal 
outcome tracking and triangulation system for the Government of Maharashtra. 

I will now hand over to my teammate [Name of Member 1] to walk you through our 
multi-channel re-engagement and 3-way verification architecture."

SPEAKER 2: MEMBER 1 (TECH & VERIFICATION) — TIME: 2:00 to 4:30

[SLIDE 3, 4 & 5: RE-ENGAGEMENT, TRIANGULATION & DEMO]

"Thank you, [Leader's Name].

To ensure we never lose contact with trainees like Swapnil over a continuous 3-year 
horizon, Skill Sync eliminates all app-download friction using two powerful channels:

1. AUTOMATED WHATSAPP BOT (Marathi, Hindi, English):
   Every 3, 6, 12, and 24 months, Swapnil receives an automated WhatsApp survey with 
   interactive quick-reply buttons. He taps 'Promoted / Salary Hike', types his new 
   salary of Rs 24,000, and submits it in 10 seconds. He earns SkillCoins reward points 
   for keeping his records updated!

2. MARATHI AI IVR VOICE AGENT FOR RURAL KEYPAD PHONES:
   For rural and tribal districts like Gadchiroli and Nandurbar where trainees have basic 
   2G phones with no internet, our automated voice agent calls them in Marathi. They answer 
   simple keypad prompts (e.g. Press 1 for job, Press 2 for self-employed), which logs 
   directly into our database.

3. 3-WAY SIGNAL TRIANGULATION (Zero Fake Placements):
   We never rely on self-reporting alone. We cross-verify every claim across 3 signals:
   - Signal A: Trainee Self-Report (WhatsApp / Voice)
   - Signal B: Employer 1-Click HRMS Verification (HR confirms role and salary in 1 click)
   - Signal C: EPFO / UAN Provident Fund Proxy (for formal jobs) or Udyam MSME Registry 
     (for self-employed micro-enterprises).
   When all three signals match, the record achieves a 100% Triangulated Trust Index.

4. 100% DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE:
   Under Indian privacy laws, we never store raw Aadhaar numbers. All records use masked 
   IDs (XXXXXXXX7821) and cryptographic tokens, giving citizens full rights to manage and 
   revoke their tracking consent.

I will now hand over to [Name of Member 2] to explain our Predictive AI engine and 
state-wide policy impact."

[SLIDE 6, 7 & 8: PREDICTIVE AI, NLP REFORM & STATE IMPACT]

"Thank you, [Member 1's Name].

Beyond just tracking historical data, Skill Sync uses artificial intelligence to 
actively protect government investments:

1. PREDICTIVE ATTRITION AI MODEL (Preventing Job Drop-Outs):
   Using a Random Forest classifier, our AI analyzes 7 friction factors: salary, commute 
   distance, night shifts, contract type, and course relevance. 
   When the AI noticed Swapnil was traveling 35 km for a Rs 14,000 wage on night shifts, 
   it calculated a 78% High Attrition Risk. 
   Before Swapnil could quit, the system alerted his ITI counselor with specific actions: 
   provide a transport allowance and schedule a counseling call. Swapnil stayed in his career!

2. NLP TOPIC CLUSTERING FOR SYLLABUS UPGRADES:
   Our Natural Language Processing engine analyzes feedback from employers across Pune, 
   Nashik, and Aurangabad. It discovered over 300 automotive companies complaining about a 
   lack of 'EV Battery BMS Diagnostics'. The AI automatically flags this to MSIS so the state 
   can modernize the ITI curriculum with hands-on EV modules.

3. STATEWIDE PROVEN IMPACT (Our Dataset):
   - 3,43,750 Trainees tracked across all 36 Maharashtra districts.
   - 91.0% Certification Rate.
   - 71.6% Verified 6-Month Retention & 65.3% 12-Month Retention.
   - 1.73x Average Wage Multiplier (starting stipend of Rs 14,500 growing to Rs 25,000+).
   - 90.6% Triangulated Trust Index.
   - 23.1% Self-Employment Rate in tribal and rural regions.

CONCLUSION:
Skill Sync transforms skilling governance in Maharashtra from a blind numbers game into an 
intelligent, evidence-based livelihood engine. 

Thank you, and we are now ready for your questions!"
