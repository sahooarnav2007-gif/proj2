Master Script

The 3-Speaker Presentation Script (Exact Words to Speak)

Speaker 1: Team Leader (You) Time: 0:00 - 2:00 (2 Minutes)

Role: The Visionary & Problem Definer (Opening Hook, Ground Reality of Maharashtra, The 80% Drop-off, Solution Overview)

"Respected Judges, every year the Government of Maharashtra invests hundreds of crores to train lakhs of youth through ITIs, PMKVA, and state skilling initiatives. Our portals record enrollment, classroom attendance, and certification well — but the moment a student graduates, the government loses contact with over 80% of trainees within 6 months.

Meet Swapnil, a 20-year-old student from rural Nashik who completed an ITI certification in EV maintenance. On graduation day, the state marked him as 'successfully skilled'. But within 3 months, Swapnil changed his SIM card, migrated to Pune for work, got burned out on night shifts, and silently quit his job. His employer noticed he lacked modern EV battery diagnostic skills, but had no way to report this back to the government in Mantralaya.

To eliminate this data black hole, our team built SKILL SYNC — an AI-powered longitudinal outcome tracking and triangulation platform for the Government of Maharashtra. I will now hand over to my teammate [Member 1] to walk you through our multi-channel re-engagement and 3-way verification architecture."
Speaker 2: Member 1 (Tech & Verification Specialist) Time: 2:00 - 4:30 (2.5 Minutes)

Role: The Data Integrity Engineer (WhatsApp Bot, Marathi Voice IVR, 3-Way Triangulation, DPDP 2023 Privacy)

"Thank you, [Leader's Name]. To ensure we never lose contact with trainees like Swapnil across a continuous 3-year horizon, Skill Sync eliminates all app-download friction using two powerful channels:

1. Automated WhatsApp Bot (Marathi, Hindi, English): Every 3, 6, 12, and 24 months, Swapnil receives an automated WhatsApp survey with interactive quick-reply buttons. He taps 'Promoted / Salary Hike', enters his new salary of ₹24,000, and submits it in 10 seconds. He earns SkillCoins reward points for keeping his records updated!
2. Marathi AI Voice Calls (IVR) for Rural Keypad Phones: For rural/tribal districts like Gadchiroli and Nandurbar where youth have basic 2G phones with no internet, our automated voice agent calls them in Marathi. They answer simple keypad prompts (Press 1 for job, Press 2 for self-employed), logging directly into the state database.
3. 3-Way Signal Triangulation (Zero Fake Placements): We cross-verify every claim across 3 signals: (A) Trainee Self-Report, (B) Employer 1-Click HRMS confirmation, and (C) EPFO / UAN Provident Fund or Udyam MSME Registry. When all 3 match, the record achieves a 100% Triangulated Trust Score.
4. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance: We never store raw Aadhaar numbers (masked as XXXXXXXX7821) and all identifiers use cryptographic tokens.

I will now hand over to [Member 2] to explain our Predictive AI engine and state-wide policy impact."
Speaker 3: Member 2 (AI & Analytics Specialist) Time: 4:30 - 7:00 (2.5 Minutes)

Role: The Data Scientist & Policy Strategist (Predictive Attrition AI, NLP Syllabus Mining, 36-District Impact & ROI)

"Thank you, [Member 1's Name]. Beyond tracking historical data, Skill Sync uses artificial intelligence to actively protect government skilling investments:

1. Predictive Attrition AI Model (Preventing Job Drop-Outs): Using a Random Forest classifier, our AI analyzes 7 friction factors: salary, commute distance, night shifts, contract type, and course relevance. When the AI noticed Swapnil was traveling 35 km for a ₹14,000 wage on night shifts, it calculated a 78% High Attrition Risk. Before Swapnil could quit, the system alerted his ITI counselor with actionable guidance: provide a transport allowance and schedule a counseling session. Swapnil stayed in his career!
2. NLP Topic Clustering for Syllabus Upgrades: Our Natural Language Processing engine analyzes feedback from employers across Pune, Nashik, and Aurangabad. It discovered over 300 automotive companies complaining about a lack of 'EV Battery BMS Diagnostics'. The AI automatically flags this to MSIS so the state can modernize the ITI curriculum with hands-on EV modules.
3. Statewide Proven Impact: We model 3,43,750 Trainees across all 36 Maharashtra districts, achieving a 91.0% Certification Rate, a 71.6% 6-Month Verified Retention, and an average 1.73x Wage Multiplier.

Skill Sync transforms skilling governance in Maharashtra from a blind numbers game into an intelligent, evidence-based livelihood engine. Thank you, and we are now ready for your questions!"
